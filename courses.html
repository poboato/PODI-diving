Our "Training" Programs

At PODI, we believe learning should be traumatic, permanent, and filmed for content. Every course is designed to maximize your financial outlay while minimizing your life expectancy. Our curriculum has been described by maritime safety experts as "a series of poor decisions in sequence." We took that as a compliment.

⚠ IMPORTANT: PODI courses do not meet any international diving standards. They don't meet local standards either. They barely meet the standard of "being a thing that happens." Completion of any PODI course does not qualify you to dive anywhere that requires proof of training. It qualifies you to dive with us. That's almost worse.

Entry Level

🫧

Bubblemaker INDUSTRIAL APPLIANCES

We duct-tape a regulator to your face and toss you into a running commercial washing machine on the heavy-duty cycle. If you surface with all your teeth and no broken bones, you pass. The spin cycle teaches buoyancy control through pure terror. Past students describe the experience as "like being born again, but angrier."

☠ Learning Objectives
  • Overcome fear of enclosed spinning environments
  • Develop jaw strength for regulator retention during centrifugal force
  • Master rapid breathing in high-G, low-dignity scenarios
  • Learn to distinguish "machine noise" from "your bones breaking"
📅 Schedule 2 sessions: "The Wash" (heavy duty, hot, 45 min) • "The Rinse" (gentle cycle, cold, recovery)
🎒 What to Bring Mouthguard (teeth recommended but not required after last semester) • Change of clothes (you will be wet, emotionally and physically)
⚠ Prerequisites None. Must fit in a standard washing machine. Height limit: 5'8". Weight limit: "Is the machine rated for this?"

Ice Diver ONE HOLE. UNMARKED. FIND IT.

We cut a single hole in a frozen lake. We do not mark it with a flag. We do not tie a reference line. We push you in and walk back to the heated shack.
