Fleet Safety Statistics

The Passiver Fleet tracks all incidents, near-incidents, incidents-that-could-have-been-incidents, and incidents that were technically someone else's fault. Here are our official numbers as of the last time someone remembered to update the clipboard (the clipboard has been missing since 2023, the numbers are from memory).

47
Total Fleet Voyages

"Voyage" defined as any time a boat left the dock, regardless of whether it returned to the same dock, a different dock, any dock at all, or a country we cannot legally re-enter.

12
Voyages Without Incident

"Incident" defined as "something requiring paperwork." We do not fill out paperwork. Therefore: 12 clean voyages. The other 35 may also have been clean. We weren't keeping track.

93%
Passenger Return Rate

93% of booked passengers return to shore. 7% are a combination of "decided to stay," "are still out there somewhere," and "we're not entirely sure they were ever on the boat in the first place." The math checks out. We checked. The math. We checked the math. We are confident the numbers add to 100%.

Life Jackets That Fit Adults

Remaining 66% are children's sizes labeled "AGES 4-6," inflatable pool rings from Dollar General, or "things we think could work" such as empty plastic bottles taped to a pool noodle. We tested the pool noodle bottle system. It did not work. We use it anyway.

Average Engines Per Boat

In 41 out of 47 recorded voyages, at least one passenger has audibly stated the words "this is fine." In 6 of those cases, it was through tears. In 2 cases, the person was Skip.
