PODI is proud to be fully accredited by the International Association of Self-Accredited Organizations (IASO), which we founded in 2023 and which currently has one member: us. Our accreditation was awarded unanimously by our board, which consists of Kyle, a mannequin named Bubbles, and a seagull that lives near the shop.
