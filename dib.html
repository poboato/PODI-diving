The Pre-Dive Sequence

Before entering the water, a DIB diver completes the following sequence in order. The sequence may not be abbreviated. The sequence may not be reordered. Deviation from the sequence requires the entire sequence to be restarted from the beginning. This has happened. We do not discuss the incident.

1
The Pre-Briefing Briefing
4 minutes. A briefing about the briefing that will follow. Covers the structure, timing, and tone of the upcoming briefing. Interruptions are documented. Questions are deferred to the next briefing.
2
The Pre-Dive Briefing
12 minutes. Gas plan. Turn pressures. Exit strategy. Buddy assignments. Buddy assignments are final and may not be renegotiated underwater. Renegotiation attempts will be noted in your permanent file.
3
The Gear Check
8 minutes. Each diver checks their own gear. Then their buddy's gear. Then their own gear again, because their buddy might have missed something. Then their buddy's gear again, because turnabout is a pillar of The Standard. Then their own gear one more time, out of distrust that has by this point become mutual.
4
The S-Drill Confirmation
3 minutes. Practice long hose deployment on land. A minimum of 47 repetitions is recommended. The 48th is optional but encouraged. Competency is judged by a DIB instructor who is watching from at least 5 metres away, arms crossed.
5
The Pre-Water Briefing
2 minutes. A briefing confirming that all previous briefings were briefed correctly. Any errors detected here restart the sequence from Step 1. This occurred seven times in 2025. Two of those were the same dive.
6
Entry
Enter the water. Feet first. Controlled. No splashing. Splashing is a Class 1 stroke indicator. A giant stride is acceptable. A giant stride that produces spray is not. We can tell the difference.
7
Pre-Descent Bubble Check
Descend to 3 metres. Stop. Look at each other. Give the "OK" sign. "OK" does not mean OK. "OK" means "I have completed all prior steps and am prepared to descend." Descend only after mutual OK is acknowledged. The mutual OK has its own sequence. That sequence has a briefing.
Estimated total: 29 minutes. Weather, gear malfunctions, or existential doubt may extend this. The record is 1 hour 47 minutes. The dive was cancelled. The briefing was considered a success.
